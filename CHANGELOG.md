# Changelog

## V3 (11 November 2026)

**Movable panels (desktop)**
- Every area is a panel you can drag, resize (snapping to a grid), collapse or close. Case files, City map, Evidence wall and Timeline can all be open at once.
- A Panels menu; named layouts; layout files to move layouts between computers; reset to default. Your layout is remembered between games, separately for police and killer screens. Phones keep the one-column layout.

**Killer mode overhaul**
- Go anywhere: watch any place, walk a district's streets watching houses, or watch a person. Act at a place, on someone's way home, or at their home. Travel time counts.
- Means: free, found, or bought (costs money and time, may leave a shop record). Each takes a different time and leaves different traces.
- Precautions: gloves, mask, shoe covers, a change of clothes.
- Deal with what's left: get rid of means, clothes or kept items in five ways, each with its own chance of discovery; plant items on others; leave them at later scenes; send them to the press or a victim's family.
- Wealth from indigent to wealthy, plus money: job pay, begging, theft, robbery, stolen cards and pawned goods, all with trails.
- Police officer as a job: inside information, and steering the case at the risk of internal affairs.
- Desires from your background that shift with what you do; a pattern panel; lists of victims and things; your stance on blood samples and interviews.
- A journal, short or full, flat and clinical.

**Computer police**
- They see where you've been (card payments, CCTV, staff memory), profile where you live from the scenes, run blood screening drives, and check shop records and bank-card use. Found items are followed up.
- In killer mode, a conviction needs at least one piece of hard evidence.
- Fixes the V2 problem where a killer acting every night at random was never noticed.

## V2 (10 November 2026)

**Play either side**
- **Killer mode:** you play the killer and the computer runs the police. Pick from 3 people, take a random one, or build your own. You have time-based days, watch places and people, plan nights with a care level, and choose signatures, keepsakes and hiding places. The urge rises daily and is settled more by acts that fit your pattern. Permanent choices: destroying evidence, writing to the newspaper, confiding in someone. You see only clinical news reports and signs that the police are close. The score is based on days free, quiet days, consistency and risky choices, with no points per victim. Easy, Normal and Hard computer police.
- **Two-player mode** on one computer, with a hand-over screen between turns.

**Setting up a case**
- Population from a village of 100 to a city of 10,000. Districts, places, jobs and police force scale with it.
- Difficulty: Easy, Normal or Hard.
- A tutorial case with step-by-step tips.
- Save / Load: 3 browser slots, plus save files.

**Your department**
- The police force grows with the population (about 1 detective per 500 people). Villages get help from county forensics, and their scenes stay fresh longer.
- Squads and standing orders ("★ Newest case").
- A career roster: detectives carry over between cases, improve with use, and can burn out or retire.
- Fatigue, a budget, a profiler, temporary officers, and paying to rush lab results.

**Investigation**
- Lab delays for blood and samples.
- Witnesses can be wrong; every report shows its confidence.
- Victims' friends, family and coworkers can be interviewed. Phone records.
- Background checks: hometowns, earlier names, old records, and people from the same town.
- Old incident reports, including the killer's earlier acts.
- The killer's origin story, and people from the killer's past who live in the city.
- Households that grow suspicious, cover up, contradict alibis or call the tip line.
- Hiding places: home, garage, car, work locker, storage unit (found through finances), or buried outside.

**The press and the city**
- Choose what to release at press conferences. Releasing the signature makes copycats possible.
- The press gives the killer a nickname.
- Frightened districts: fewer people out at night, more witnesses.
- Neighbourhood patrols.

**Screens**
- A Timeline tab, notes on cases and people (also shown on the evidence wall), the map in killer mode, and a layout for phones and tablets.

## V1 (9 November 2026)

First playable prototype.

**City**
- 2,000 residents, each with a name, age, home district, job, shifts and work days, evening habits with set nights, height, shoe size, hair colour, blood type, car, household and a short life story.
- 6 districts and 55 places (bars, diners, gyms, parks, a church, a hospital, factories, offices, schools and more).

**The killer**
- Created fresh for every case: a victim type, two hunting places with set nights, a method, a signature item left at every scene, and trophies taken from victims.
- Strikes on a shrinking cycle and leaves physical traces (shoe print, blood, hair, wound angle, witness sightings).
- Changes behaviour when the police get close: stops for a while, moves to the other hunting place, wears gloves.

**Your department**
- Four detectives with different levels of Forensics, People and Surveillance skill.
- Tasks: examine a scene, attend an autopsy, canvass witnesses, pull a place's staff and regulars list, interview, check alibis, request a blood sample, night stakeout at a place, tail a person, canvass a map area, and seek a warrant to search a home.
- Free search of public city records.
- The Chief can hold a press conference every 3 days to ease pressure and bring in tips.
- Charge a suspect and the court decides. Convicting the wrong person comes back to haunt you.
- You lose if public pressure reaches 100.

**Screens**
- Case files with each victim's full life and the evidence found.
- City map with places, homes, bodies, last-seen lines and people of interest. Draw circles to canvass areas.
- Area canvasses take about one detective-day per 60 homes. Detectives can share the work, and you can stop and resume. They turn up residents, night sightings, doorbell-camera footage and neighbourhood gossip (some true, much of it not).
- Evidence wall: draggable cards for victims, people of interest, places and the killer, joined by red (points to guilt), green (points away) and grey (plain link) strings. Click a card to follow its strings.
- 📌 marks people of interest wherever their name appears.
