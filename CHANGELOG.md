# Changelog

## V1 (9 November 2026)

First playable prototype.

**City**
- 2,000 residents, each with a name, age, home district, job, shifts and work days, evening habits with set nights, height, shoe size, hair colour, blood type, car, household and a short life story.
- 6 districts and 55 places (bars, diners, gyms, parks, a church, a hospital, factories, offices, schools and more).

**The killer**
- Created fresh for every case: a victim type, two hunting places with set nights, a method, a signature item left at every scene, and trophies taken from victims.
- Strikes on a shrinking cycle and leaves physical traces (shoe print, blood, hair, wound angle, witness sightings).
- Changes behaviour when the police get close: stops for a while, moves to the other hunting place, wears gloves.

**Your department**
- Four detectives with different levels of Forensics, People and Surveillance skill.
- Tasks: examine a scene, attend an autopsy, canvass witnesses, pull a place's staff and regulars list, interview, check alibis, request a blood sample, night stakeout at a place, tail a person, canvass a map area, and seek a warrant to search a home.
- Free search of public city records.
- The Chief can hold a press conference every 3 days to ease pressure and bring in tips.
- Charge a suspect and the court decides. Convicting the wrong person comes back to haunt you.
- You lose if public pressure reaches 100.

**Screens**
- Case files with each victim's full life and the evidence found.
- City map with places, homes, bodies, last-seen lines and people of interest. Draw circles to canvass areas.
- Area canvasses take about one detective-day per 60 homes. Detectives can share the work, and you can stop and resume. They turn up residents, night sightings, doorbell-camera footage and neighbourhood gossip (some true, much of it not).
- Evidence wall: draggable cards for victims, people of interest, places and the killer, joined by red (points to guilt), green (points away) and grey (plain link) strings. Click a card to follow its strings.
- 📌 marks people of interest wherever their name appears.
