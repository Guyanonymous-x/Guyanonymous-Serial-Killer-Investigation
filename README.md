# Guyanonymous' Serial Killer Investigation

A turn-based investigation and management game. A serial killer is loose in a simulated place of 100 to 10,000 people. Run the police department that has to find them, play the killer against computer-run police, or play one against the other on one computer.

Every resident has a name, an age, a home, a job with set shifts and days, evening habits (which bar, gym or church, and on which nights), a look (height, hair, shoe size), a blood type and maybe a car. The killer is one of them. They have a hidden type of victim, two places where they hunt on set nights, a signature they leave at every scene, and they change their habits when they sense the police getting close.

The victims are people too. Their lives are the main clue: where they spent their evenings, and on which nights, is what links them.

## How to play

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). Double-clicking it is enough. There's nothing to install, and it works offline (only the fonts need the internet).
2. Start a new case: choose Police, Killer or Two-player, the population and the difficulty. In police mode, give each detective a task each day, then press **End day**.
3. Use the **Case files**, **City map** and **Evidence wall** tabs to work out who the killer is.
4. Charge your suspect when the case is strong enough. A court decides.

Press **How to play** in the game for the full rules. New players can start with the **tutorial case** on the new-case screen. Every rule and number is listed in [docs/DESIGN_V2.md](docs/DESIGN_V2.md) (V2 changes and additions) and [docs/DESIGN.md](docs/DESIGN.md) (the V1 base rules).
