# V3 plan (11 November 2026)

**Status:** built in V3 (11 November 2026). The rules as built, with all numbers, are in `DESIGN_V3.md`.

- Items marked **Decided** are your decisions.
- Items marked **Proposed** are Claude's ideas, and need your OK at a checkpoint.
- Everything to do with killings stays clinical and non-graphic. Means and precautions are game categories, never real-world technique.

V3 is one release with two parts: **A, panels** (built first) and **B, the killer-mode overhaul**.

---

## A. Movable panels

**Decided**
- Every panel is a window. You drag it by its title bar, resize it from its corner, and collapse it to just its title.
- **Snap to a grid** keeps edges lined up.
- Case files, City map, Evidence wall and Timeline become **4 separate panels**, so all of them can be open at once.
- **Saving layouts:**
  - your last layout is remembered automatically and used in every new game
  - layouts can be saved under names (for example "Big monitor" and "Laptop")
  - a layout can be exported and imported as a file
  - one button resets to the default layout
- **Killer mode gets movable panels too.** Police and killer layouts are saved separately.

**Proposed**
- A 20-pixel grid.
- The default layout is fitted to your screen size the first time.
- A **Panels** menu reopens closed panels.
- Phones keep the simple one-column layout.

## B. Killer-mode overhaul

### B1. Police fixes (Decided: all four)

- **Being seen where you act:** card payments, CCTV or staff memory can put you on that place's records for that night.
- **Geographic profiling:** the police estimate the killer's home area from the spread of the scenes, and canvass there.
- **Blood screening drives:** once the blood type is known and pressure is high, the police ask people in an area for samples. Refusing is noticed.
- **The cost of acting every night:** fatigue, a short period after each act when the urge doesn't rise, and more general signs (more police around, patrols, news of the investigation).

### B2. Going anywhere (Decided)

- Watch **any** place: businesses, workplaces, parks, a street or a particular house. Not only your own usual places.
- Act **between places**: on someone's way home from work, or from a bar or park.

### B3. Means (Decided)

- Means are game categories, e.g. bare hands, cord, blade, blunt object, poison, vehicle.
- Some are **free** (hands, or something at home or work). Others must be **obtained**, which costs free hours and money and may leave a record. **Wealth** limits what you can get.
- Means can be **improvised** from the scene: quick, but the traces are unpredictable.
- Each means takes a **different amount of time** to use, which changes the hours needed and the witness risk.
- A means can be **kept and reused** (evidence if it's found) or **disposed of**.

### B4. Precautions and disposal (Decided)

- Buy or use precautions, such as gloves, a mask or a change of clothes. Each lowers certain traces and costs time or money.
- Dispose of means and of clothes with evidence on them (burn, bin, river, bury, and so on). The chance of discovery depends on how and where.

### B5. Wealth and money (Decided)

- Five wealth levels: indigent, poor, middle, comfortable, wealthy. They affect your car, your home, free time, storage units, travel, what you can buy, and how the police and press treat you.
- An actual **money** amount.
  - **Income:** pay from your job; the indigent can beg during the day.
  - **Extra money** comes from risky actions such as theft, or robbing victims (a wallet or purse, or valuables if at their home).
  - Using a stolen card or selling stolen items can leave a trail for the police.

### B6. Police officer as a job (Decided: all four effects)

- **Inside information:** see some of what the police know.
- **Steer the case:** misplace evidence, or point colleagues at others. Every attempt risks discovery.
- **Extra scrutiny:** if you're suspected, internal affairs moves fast and hard.
- **Less free time:** long, irregular shifts.

### B7. Souvenirs and items (Decided)

- Lists of your victims and of everything you took.
- What you can do with a taken item:
  - **plant it** on someone (their home, car or bin) to point the police at them; risky if you're seen
  - **leave it at a later scene**, to link cases on purpose
  - **dump it** somewhere; it may be found later, with traces on it
  - **send it** to the press or to a victim's family; a strong clue for the police

### B8. Desires and pattern (Decided: both)

- Your background sets 1–2 **desires** (a type of person, a kind of place, a time of night). Over time they shift toward what you actually do.
- Acts that follow your desires settle the urge the most.
- A **pattern panel** shows the pattern you've been building.

### B9. Narrative (Decided: both, with a toggle)

- Short clinical case-file entries each day by default.
- A fuller daily journal in a flat, detached voice, switched on with a toggle.
- Both stay clinical and non-graphic.
